The Program

Traditional Taekwondo training

KMA's Taekwondo program covers the fundamentals of the Korean martial art — stances, blocks, kicks, forms (poomsae), and sparring.

Classes build coordination, flexibility, and cardiovascular conditioning while teaching the discipline and respect that define traditional Taekwondo.

Open to adults and kids. Contact us to arrange a trial class and we'll match you with the right coach and schedule.
