About Brendo

Competitor and instructor

Brendo Pudan is a BJJ competitor and instructor at KMA, representing Team Fabricio Philippines. A gold medalist in Philippine BJJ competition, Brendo brings proven competitive skills to his coaching.

At KMA, Brendo teaches BJJ — working with students from beginners through to advanced practitioners. His no-gi game is particularly sharp, honed through regular competition on the national circuit.

Brendo's teaching style is direct and technique-focused. Whether you're learning your first submission or refining your competition strategy, he meets you where you are and helps you level up.
